# Manuscript novelty revision checklist

Before submission, the authors must manually verify and cite prior work on:

1. early Fish4Knowledge trajectory-disjoint or recorded-group-aware evaluation;
2. foreground/mask-guided background robustness;
3. foreground/background recombination and context intervention;
4. training-time segmentation or mask privilege;
5. context removal and context debiasing;
6. CLIB and subject/non-primary contrastive controls.

The manuscript should not claim that it introduces group-disjoint evaluation to
Fish4Knowledge or that foreground supervision itself is novel. Safer wording is
that the study provides a modern quantitative audit of frame-level versus
recorded-group-disjoint evaluation and integrates it with explicit context
interventions, robustness-oriented inference, and a label-level
foreground-sufficiency instantiation. The contribution is study-level
integration and evidence design, not an exclusive claim to each component.
